## Cross-version boundary

The official Steam Patch 1.0.10 notes, published after the captured 1.0.9 build, specifically state that the developers fixed a state where dying to Tuna, spawning Albatross, and then being unable to respawn could break progression. The public guide may explain this fix and advise updating before retrying. It must not imply that every physics trick or weapon-switch cancel from 1.0.9 is guaranteed in later builds.

## Safe article conclusions

1. Rocks is a compact vertical island: identify the upper bait/cashier building, lower shop, grill awning, dock, grass landing lane, and at least one doorway before casting.
2. Keep the ordinary Professional Lure pool separate from the Professional Boss Lure route.
3. Tuna has health plus a white escape/time bar, charges aggressively, and can become airborne. A doorway/structure setup is safer than depending on a last-second dodge.
4. Keep the first defeated Tuna intact for the Albatross dependency.
5. Albatross is a ranged aerial encounter. Its passes and white falling projectiles make open ground dangerous; a roof, awning, or doorway creates safer firing windows.
6. Alternating two ranged weapons can reduce downtime in the captured build, but this is optional, expensive, and version-sensitive.
7. The new recording does not clearly show the Albatross Head UI, accepted Head hand-in, or Volcano coordinate confirmation. Retain those steps only from the separately corroborated route evidence and label the completion test clearly.
