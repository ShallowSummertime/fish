## Timeline and observed route

The recording contains two overlapping walkthrough passes. The later pass (roughly 12:30 onward) shows the clearest Island 4 and Island 5 sequence.

### Rocks / Island 4

1. **12:45–12:55 — shop preparation.** The Patch 1.0.5 shop visibly offers a bullet upgrade and Suppressor for equipped ranged weapons. The narration recommends a numeric damage target, but that number is historical and is not safe to publish as a current requirement.
2. **13:00–13:20 — cooking and cash.** A Red Snapper is grilled from 1.46x to 1.50x and removed at the cap. The 1.50x cap is independently current-cross-checked before publication.
3. **13:30–14:10 — Tuna.** The Professional Boss Lure starts the Tuna encounter. The HUD shows an orange health bar and a separate white escape/time bar. Tuna attacks with jumping/body movement on land.
4. **15:50–17:50 — Albatross.** The defeated Tuna draws the bird. The player fights from the red shop/grill awning, exposing only long enough to fire a scoped rifle. The bird has a health bar and a separate white escape/time bar. The visible safe loop is cover, observe the pass, fire during the reset, and return under the roof.
5. **18:10–18:25 — unique drop and departure.** The route keeps the Albatross Head separate from ordinary meat, carries it back to the route NPC, and then departs with the next coordinates.

### Volcano / Island 5

1. **18:40–19:20 — arrival and scientist.** The player follows Radar/navigation to a dark volcanic island. A yellow-suited Scientist stands beside the shop. Scientific Lure is visibly listed at $500 in Patch 1.0.5.
2. **19:30–20:00 — five-fish hand-in.** The player fishes the Scientific pool, then approaches the Scientist with five fish/creatures in inventory. Current written sources differ on whether any five fish count or five native Scientific catches are required. The public guide recommends five fresh Scientific catches as the safest route and explains the discrepancy.
3. **20:10–20:55 — Bowhead Whale.** Fish Bucket starts the Bowhead Whale encounter. The whale repeatedly becomes airborne and body-slams/charges; the recording advises running when it jumps.
4. **21:00–21:15 — whole-body carry.** The defeated Bowhead body is kept intact and brought to the crater/lava area. It is a progression object, not sale or cooking stock.
5. **21:25–22:35 — Mutated Bowhead Whale.** The lava/crater transformation starts the final fight. The mutated whale leaps and charges while lava patches divide the arena. The recording repeatedly warns not to step in lava and demonstrates moving first, firing after an attack pass, and keeping distance.
6. **22:40 onward — finish.** The Whale Fin is the protected hand-in item. Current written sources cross-check the Scientist hand-in and RHIB/military-boat key ending. Later footage also revisits optional/postgame areas; those are not required for the five-location story route.
